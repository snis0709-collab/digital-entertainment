Music

Music Streaming

Services such as Spotify changed how people listen to music. Songs are available instantly, and playlists make music personal and easy to discover.

Video

Video Streaming

Video platforms gave people access to films, shows, and documentaries anytime. This changed how people watch media and reduced the need for physical copies.

Online

Online Video

Videos shared online became a major part of modern entertainment. Creators could publish content directly to people all over the world.

Platform

Digital Entertainment Platforms

Streaming platforms combine music, video, gaming, and live content in one place. They now shape how digital entertainment is shared and enjoyed.
